Privacy and terms

Stilltype keeps what it needs to show your progress and run races, and nothing else. No ads, no analytics, no selling.

What is stored

Guests

Nothing on our side. Without an account, your runs stay in this tab and are gone when you leave.

Accounts

Your email address, and one profile holding your settings, your newest 24 full runs and up to 1,000 run summaries. It is stored with Supabase, and only your signed-in account can read it. Signing in with Google shares your email address with us and nothing else.

Races and friends

During a race the server holds the room, the names you chose, what each of you has typed and the score. Rooms are deleted a day after they expire, and race results never enter your profile. Your friend list is the names and emails you saved, and only you can see it.

Suggestions

A feature suggestion is saved with the screen you sent it from, never with your account. Nobody can read suggestions back through the public API.

This device

Your browser keeps your sign-in, a copy of your profile and your inbox so the app works offline. Signing out removes the inbox from this device.

Terms of use

Stilltype is free and offered as is, without any guarantee. Keep race names decent, and do not script races or flood the service. You can clear your history from Progress at any time. Deleting an account is not self-serve yet.
