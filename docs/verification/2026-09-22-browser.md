## Limits and next validation

This is one browser and desktop keyboard path, with responsive viewport checks, all on the pre-redesign build. Actual mobile keyboards, assistive technology and IME composition need device testing. Afterimage input-to-visible-feedback timing is recorded separately in `docs/verification/2026-09-22-latency.md`. No learning efficacy, server-backed multiplayer, public ranking integrity or deployment is claimed.

The original quote corpus is deliberately small. Generated assessments match configured mode/length/punctuation/corpus/metric conditions but do not guarantee matched target frequency; target samples are shown and small samples remain inconclusive.
